A November trip, three ways

Which national park fits your November plans?

Start with the trip you can actually take. These are three different editorial picks from the parks suggested below, not a forecast or a promise of current access.

One park day · Tucson, Arizona

Saguaro

Choose it for a short Sonoran Desert visit with cactus landscapes and walks. The park has east and west districts on opposite sides of Tucson, so choose your stops before setting out.

Plan ahead: Check the NPS conditions and allow time between districts.

Two park days · California/Nevada

Death Valley

Choose it for a longer desert landscape trip. NPS identifies November through March as its best hiking season, especially compared with dangerous summer heat at lower elevations.

Plan ahead: Check NPS hiking and road guidance before choosing a route.

One park day · Kentucky

Mammoth Cave

Choose it for a cave-focused trip instead of a desert one. Cave tours run in November, but the routes and departure times vary by date.

Plan ahead: A ticket is required to enter the cave, and NPS strongly recommends reserving a tour.
