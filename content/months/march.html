A March trip, three ways

Which national park fits your March plans?

March can mean dry-season wildlife, spring canyon scenery, or a short desert visit. Pick the kind of trip you want, then check current conditions before booking.

Two park days · South Florida

Everglades

Choose it for wetlands and wildlife viewing. March falls in the park's dry season, when NPS reports milder weather, fewer mosquitoes, and abundant wildlife.

Plan ahead: The park has several distant entrances. Use the NPS dry-season guide to choose an area and activity.

Two park days · Southwest Utah

Zion

Choose it for canyon scenery and a longer walking trip. Spring brings warming days, but March can be busy and cold at night; snowmelt may close the Narrows.

Plan ahead: Check NPS spring guidance and current trail and shuttle conditions before picking hikes.

One park day · Tucson, Arizona

Saguaro

Choose it for a compact Sonoran Desert trip with cactus landscapes and scenic walks. Its east and west districts are about 30 miles apart, so one day calls for a selective plan.

Plan ahead: Check the NPS district guide. Bloom timing varies with species and weather.
